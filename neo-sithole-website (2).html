Political researcher and doctoral candidate specialising in populism, democratic governance, and electoral behaviour across Sub-Saharan Africa and Europe. Affiliated with the University of Szeged and the European Centre for Populism Studies, my work focuses on what populist movements mean for institutional trust, political culture, and regional cooperation in fragile and transitioning democracies. Peer-reviewed publications in international journals, policy briefs for academic and civic audiences, and active engagement in EU and Africa-focused policy forums complement a decade of research and editorial experience spanning both continents.
